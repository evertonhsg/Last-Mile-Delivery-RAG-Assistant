## 1. Service Tiers

Meridian Logistics operates three customer-facing service tiers for last-mile delivery:

### Priority Overnight (PO)
- **Commitment:** Delivery by 10:30 the next business day.
- **Eligible zones:** Zone 1 and Zone 2 only.
- **Surcharge:** +€4.50 per parcel vs Standard rate.
- **Failure threshold:** SLA breach if delivery occurs after 10:30.
- **Compensation:** Full refund of surcharge on breach + credit note of €2.00.

### Express (EX)
- **Commitment:** Delivery by 18:00 the next business day.
- **Eligible zones:** All zones (Zone 4 subject to volume availability).
- **Surcharge:** +€1.80 per parcel vs Standard rate.
- **Failure threshold:** SLA breach if delivery occurs after 18:00.
- **Compensation:** Refund of surcharge on breach.

### Standard (ST)
- **Commitment:** Delivery within 2 business days by 18:00.
- **Eligible zones:** All zones.
- **Surcharge:** Base rate, no surcharge.
- **Failure threshold:** SLA breach after 18:00 on day 2.
- **Compensation:** Credit note of €1.00 per parcel on breach.

Meridian Logistics sets the following internal performance targets by zone:

| Service | Zone 1 | Zone 2 | Zone 3 | Zone 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Failed Delivery Handling

### Attempt Policy
- A maximum of **3 delivery attempts** are made before a parcel is returned
  to the sender or held at a pickup point.
- Attempt intervals: day 0 (first attempt), day 1 (second), day 3 (third).
- After 3 failed attempts, parcel moves to **Hold for Pickup** status for 7 days.

### Missed Delivery Window
- If the customer is absent, a **digital notification** is triggered immediately
  via the Meridian Delivery Manager app.
- Customer has until 20:00 on the same day to reschedule via self-service.
- If not rescheduled, the next attempt is automatically booked.

### Damage or Loss
- Claims must be filed within **10 business days** of expected delivery date.
- Standard liability cap: **€100 per parcel** unless declared value is higher.
- High-value shipments (declared value >€500) require mandatory insurance.

## 4. SLA Measurement & Reporting

- **Data source:** Meridian Scan Events (out-for-delivery scan + POD scan).
- **Reporting cadence:** Daily dashboard refresh at 06:00 CET; weekly station
  manager report on Mondays.
- **Escalation trigger:** If any station drops below SLA target for 3 consecutive
  days, an automated alert is sent to the Regional Operations Manager.
- **Annual review:** SLA targets are reviewed every April in alignment with
  network capacity plans.

---

## 5. SLA Exclusions

The following conditions exempt Meridian Logistics from SLA compensation obligations:

- Severe weather events (wind >90 km/h, snowfall >15 cm in 24h, flooding).
- Industrial action affecting transport infrastructure.
- Incorrect or incomplete delivery address provided by shipper.
- Force majeure events as defined under applicable law.
- Bank holidays in the destination country (these are excluded from
  the business day count).
